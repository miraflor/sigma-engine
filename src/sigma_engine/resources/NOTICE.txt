Bundled Philippine input-output resources
==========================================

Files
-----
- psa_2018_io80.csv: 2018 benchmark 80-industry transaction matrix.
- psa_2018_io16.csv: 2018 benchmark 16-industry transaction matrix.

Rows are suppliers and columns are users. Values are in million Philippine pesos.
Sector identifiers are normalized to SIGMA's canonical 01..80 / 01..16 ordering.

Source
------
Philippine Statistics Authority, "PSA releases the 2018 Input-Output Tables",
Reference No. 2021-510, released 9 December 2021.
https://psa.gov.ph/content/psa-releases-2018-input-output-tables

The PSA page states that the 2018 benchmark accounts include transaction tables at
16x16, 80x80, and 240x240 resolution. The PSA website states that its data and content
are licensed under CC BY 4.0 unless otherwise stated.

Normalization
-------------
The packaged CSVs contain only the square intermediate transaction blocks needed by
SIGMA Engine. Presentation-only rows/columns are omitted and sector positions are
renamed to canonical two-digit identifiers. No weights are rescaled or approximated.
The IO16 and IO80 matrices have the same total intermediate use:
18,274,281.3667199 million PHP.
