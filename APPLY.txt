SIGMA Engine 0.2.7 Fast-MWAS-default overlay

Baseline
--------
Apply this overlay directly to the authoritative sigma-engine 0.2.6 tree supplied on
2026-10-01 after the Voronoi regression-test correction. Extract/copy it into the
repository root and allow replacement of matching files.

Main change
-----------
- Restricted Gradient Extraction (RGE) is removed.
- Maximum-weight acyclic subgraph (MWAS) is restored.
- Fast deterministic descending-weight cycle-avoidance MWAS is the default.
- Exact zero-gap SciPy/HiGHS MILP MWAS remains available with --mwas-method exact.
- Fast mode is explicitly reported as heuristic/non-optimal; exact mode must prove an optimum.
- sigma_io_dag.csv is written immediately after the IO-DAG stage.
- Existing clustering, center, distance, Voronoi fallback, checkpoint, and provenance annotations remain.
