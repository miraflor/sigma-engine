Behavior:
- the first matching run after this hotfix builds the retained-point augmented road graph once
  and writes it under .sigma_checkpoints/;
- later matching resumed runs restore that graph instead of repeating road augmentation;
- a missing, unreadable, misaligned, or structurally invalid augmented-road checkpoint is
  rebuilt and overwritten automatically;
- engine version remains 0.2.7 so existing clustering/center/distance/Voronoi/network-X
  checkpoints remain reusable.
